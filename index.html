A Beginner's Guide to Skateboarding




Skateboarding, form of recreation and sport, popular among youths, in which a person rides standing balance. This is an action sport, recreational activity, and mode of transportation where a person rides and performs tricks standing balanced on a small board with four wheels.

Basic Techniques
  • When falling, fall on your back or side and protect your head
  • Kickflip (the most common trick) is one of the best partytrick to learn
  • Find your flow by checking the best stance you're comfortable with
Fun Facts
  • Riding with your right foot forward is called a "goofy" stance, a term popularized by a 1937 Disney cartoon showing Goofy surfing.
  • English bulldog named Otto holds a Guinness World Record for rolling through 30 human legs on a skateboard in just 25 seconds./li>
  • Skateboarding made its official debut as a Summer Olympic sport at the 2020 Tokyo Games
Culture and Community
  • Music, fashion (like skate shoes and oversized apparel), deck art, and independent magazines.
  • Skaters view city stairs, ledges, and rails as a creative canvas rather than just concrete.
  • The history of anti-skate devices, private property bans, and the eventual rise of dedicated public skateparks.
